Good to know

Questions, answered.

Will this guarantee more patient bookings?

No system can guarantee bookings or patient numbers. This setup organizes enquiry capture and follow-up; results depend on demand, availability, staff response and the clinic’s booking process.

Do I need a new website?

Not necessarily. We confirm the best fit during intake. The starter setup may use a standalone form or connect to an existing workflow, depending on access and requirements.

Are WhatsApp or Telegram alerts included automatically?

No. The channel depends on the clinic’s existing tools, access and provider requirements. We confirm the option and costs first, and only call it working after a successful delivery test.

How is patient information handled?

We collect only the details needed for the enquiry workflow and agree who can access them. The clinic remains responsible for its patient privacy, retention and clinical record obligations.
