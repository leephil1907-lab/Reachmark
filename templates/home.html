COMMON QUESTIONS

What to know before you start.

What does the free website audit check?

It checks accessible public website signals such as contact paths, mobile experience, SEO basics, trust elements, and technical responses. Results depend on what the website makes available to the checker.

How long does it take?

The initial public check runs after submission. Timing depends on the website response and whether it limits automated access; a blocked check is reported as inconclusive rather than a confirmed fault.

Is my data private?

The initial audit checks public website information. Review our Privacy Policy for details about information handling.

Can Reachmark fix the issues?

Yes. Reachmark Digital offers scoped website fixes, local visibility setup, and ongoing growth support. Start with an audit, then discuss the fixes you want implemented.

Do I need an account?

You can run the initial public website check without creating an account. Additional workspace features and saved reports may require an account or paid plan.
