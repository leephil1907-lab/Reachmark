Where it fits

Made for the way local businesses work

Clinics, dental studios, physios

Takes the booking question at midnight and turns it into a morning appointment request.

  • “Do you take bookings online?” → explains the route, captures the request
  • “What does the first visit cost?” → quotes only published prices
  • Anything clinical → straight to a human, with the transcript

Plumbers, electricians, cleaners

On a job with dirty hands is exactly when the phone rings. The chat takes it instead.

  • “Can you come this week?” → captures area, job and callback details
  • “How much for…?” → published rates or an honest “the owner quotes that”
  • Emergencies → flags as urgent in the handoff

Lawyers, accountants, consultants

Screens the vague “quick question” and arrives with the facts sorted.

  • “Do you handle…?” → answers from the services page only
  • “Can I book a consultation?” → captures details for a callback
  • Anything resembling advice → refused, handed to the professional

Cafés, bakeries, shops

Answers the regulars’ questions while the queue is out of the door.

  • “Are you open Sunday?” → published hours, nothing assumed
  • “Do you do orders for events?” → captures date, size and contact
  • “Where are you?” → the published address, never a guess

Barbers, salons, builders, studios

Keeps the chair full and the diary honest.

  • “How much for…?” → published price list or owner quote
  • “Can I move my appointment?” → captured with name and details
  • Portfolio questions → points at real samples, never mock clients
