How payment works

1 · Choose plan & currency

Starter or Pro, in USD or NGN. You will sign in (or create a free account) first.

2 · Pay securely with Paystack

Cards and bank channels on Paystack's checkout. Reachmark never sees your card.

3 · 30 days, unlocked

Your plan activates instantly. No auto-renewal — pay again only when you want more time.

Can I switch currency or plan later?

Yes. Every payment is a fresh 30 days on whichever plan and currency you pick at checkout. A new payment extends your time from your current expiry date.

What if payment succeeds but my plan does not activate?

Paystack always confirms in the background too, so activation catches up within a minute. If not, send an enquiry with your payment reference and the studio will fix it by hand.

Is this the same as website pricing?

No. These plans unlock workspace tools. Done-for-you website builds are quoted separately — see website pricing or send an enquiry.
