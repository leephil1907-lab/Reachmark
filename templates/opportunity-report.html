How to read the opportunity score

The 0–100 score is a relative ranking of detected website gaps, not a website-quality grade, conversion probability, or revenue estimate. The base is 18 plus 9 times the sum of observed gap weights, capped at 100. Listings without an owned website have a minimum score of 78; social-only listings have a minimum of 74. When no gaps are detected, a listed website scores 12 and a listing without a website scores 64.

Priority also considers the type of gap: High is assigned at 70+ or for certain major access/website gaps; Medium at 40+ or when a commercial gap is present; otherwise Low. A check can miss content behind scripts, consent walls, bot protection, or temporary network failures. Confirm each finding manually before contacting a business. No score proves lost sales or interest in buying.

Observed page details are evidence; proposed fixes and outreach wording are recommendations. Review both before use.
