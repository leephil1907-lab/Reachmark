# Reachmark identity

**Find potential. Make your mark.**

Reachmark connects the reach of global discovery with the intention of a thoughtful first impression. The forward-R symbol uses a decisive diagonal and a destination dot to suggest finding a business and taking the next step.

## Files

- `reachmark-logo-primary.svg` / `.png`: dark wordmark on transparent background; use on warm-white/light surfaces.
- `reachmark-logo-inverse.svg` / `.png`: warm-white wordmark; use on charcoal/dark surfaces.
- `reachmark-symbol.svg` / `.png`: standalone app mark. The lime tile is part of the mark.
- `generate_assets.py`: optional reproducible source for outlined wordmarks, icons, and the social card. Run from any directory after installing `fonttools pillow cairosvg`.

SVG wordmarks contain outlines, not external font references. PNG wordmarks are supplied at 3× resolution with transparency. The app symbol PNG is 1024×1024. App-specific 192px/512px PNGs and a 1200×630 social card are in `static/`.

## Type and usage

Manrope for headings; DM Sans for interface/body copy. The wordmark is based on Manrope Bold, supplied as outlines. Font OFL notices are retained in `static/`.

Keep clear space around the logo of at least one quarter of the symbol's height. Do not stretch, add shadows to, rotate, or recolor the mark. Prefer the standalone symbol below a 140px wordmark width. Use the inverse logo only on dark surfaces; transparent white lettering will disappear on white.

The product name is a creative selection, not a trademark/domain clearance. Confirm availability in your operating markets before a commercial brand launch.
