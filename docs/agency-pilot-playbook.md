# Reachmark Agency Pilot Playbook

## Goal

Win three paying agency pilot customers, deliver the defined scope, and learn what drives continued use and renewal. This is a validation milestone, not a revenue forecast.

## Target customer

Independent web designers and small web agencies that:
- Already deliver websites or conversion-focused services.
- Want a steadier flow of qualified local-business opportunities.
- Can serve additional clients if opportunities convert.
- Will review evidence and approve outreach themselves.

## Offer under test

**Founding Agency Pilot — $299 one-time for 14 days**

- One niche and geographic market.
- Up to 25 candidate businesses reviewed.
- Up to five evidence-backed opportunity reports.
- Outreach drafts and a simple follow-up pipeline.
- One 30-minute handoff and feedback session.

Confirm scope and availability before requesting payment. Do not promise leads, replies, projects, or revenue. Exclude third-party software, data, advertising, and sending costs unless expressly included in a written agreement.

## Qualification questions

1. Which website services do you sell most often?
2. Which niche and market do you want to serve?
3. How do you currently find prospective clients?
4. What makes a prospect worth pursuing?
5. How many new client projects could you fulfil per month?
6. What would make the reports useful enough to renew?
7. Who approves outreach and handles replies?

## Pilot delivery checklist

- [ ] Record the agency's niche, market, services, and qualification criteria.
- [ ] Record the baseline process and time spent finding prospects.
- [ ] Review up to 25 candidate businesses within the agreed scope.
- [ ] Keep source URLs, check timestamps, findings, and limitations.
- [ ] Prepare up to five reports from findings that can be substantiated.
- [ ] Draft personalized outreach for agency review; do not send without approval.
- [ ] Set up the follow-up pipeline and assign next actions.
- [ ] Hold the handoff and record feedback.
- [ ] Review usage, report usefulness, time saved, replies, meetings, proposals, and wins separately.
- [ ] Ask for a renewal decision based on actual value and scope.

## Weekly acquisition experiment

Initial activity targets:
- Research 50 relevant agencies.
- Contact 40–50 qualified prospects with individually relevant messages.
- Hold at least 10 substantive customer conversations during the first month.
- Aim for three paid pilot customers; do not treat the target as a forecast.

Use appropriate channels, accurate sender information, and jurisdiction-specific marketing rules. Honor opt-outs and suppress contacts who ask not to be contacted.

## Funnel definitions

Track each stage independently:
- Researched
- Contacted
- Positive reply
- Discovery held
- Qualified
- Pilot proposed
- Paid
- Delivered
- Renewal offered
- Renewed / not renewed

A website issue is not proof of buying intent. A report is not a lead. A reply is not a meeting. A proposal is not revenue. Count revenue only when supported by actual billing and payment records.

## Decision after three pilots

Continue building only where evidence supports it:
- Which reports did customers actually use?
- Which observations led to conversations?
- How much time did delivery take?
- What was the direct cost per pilot?
- Did customers use the follow-up pipeline?
- Would they renew, at what price, and for which recurring outcome?
- Which steps should become product automation next?
