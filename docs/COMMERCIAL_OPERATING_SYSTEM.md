# Reachmark Commercial Operating System

This document turns Reachmark's product capabilities into a repeatable way to sell and deliver work. It is an operating guide, not a claim that every step is already automated.

## Positioning

**Reachmark helps businesses discover qualified prospects, understand evidence-backed website and visibility opportunities, manage approved outreach, and move customer work through proposals, payment, delivery, and retention.**

Website intelligence is the initial wedge, not the limit of the Reachmark brand. Keep lead discovery, website observations, outreach, Google Business visibility, projects, contracts, and invoicing connected without promising results the system has not measured.

## The commercial loop

1. **Discover** — save real businesses from supported sources or an import. Preserve source and location data.
2. **Verify** — inspect the public listing and website. Treat blocked, missing, or inconclusive observations as such; they are not proof of a broken website.
3. **Diagnose** — generate a Digital Opportunity Report from stored observations. Claims must have evidence and timestamps where available.
4. **Qualify** — consider service fit, contactability, evidence confidence, urgency, and whether a real buying signal exists. A gap score is not a sales forecast.
5. **Prepare** — generate a concept and draft proposal. Keep unknown prices unset; never substitute a sample price or estimated score for a quote.
6. **Approve outreach** — a human checks the recipient, lawful basis, accuracy, wording, timing, and opt-out handling before any message is sent.
7. **Respond** — record the actual reply and classify intent. Suggestions are drafts; no automatic response or promise should be made.
8. **Scope and agree** — define deliverables, exclusions, timeline, price, revision limit, client responsibilities, payment milestones, and acceptance criteria in writing.
9. **Invoice and confirm** — issue a branded invoice. Mark Paid only after payment is confirmed against the actual payment record.
10. **Deliver** — use a project checklist, keep the client informed, document handover, and record completion.
11. **Measure and retain** — record observed outcomes with a source and date. Offer maintenance or further work only when it fits the client's needs.

## Offer catalogue

Treat these as service categories, not automatically published price promises:

- **Website Opportunity Audit** — evidence-backed diagnosis and prioritized recommendations.
- **Website / Landing Page Build** — agreed pages, responsive implementation, integrations, content responsibilities, revisions, and launch support.
- **Local Visibility Support** — authorized Google Business Profile review, listing hygiene, review-response workflow, and reporting.
- **Prospect Research and Outreach Operations** — qualified lists, evidence-backed drafts, suppression handling, and human-approved sends.
- **Ongoing Growth Support** — agreed reporting, website maintenance, local visibility work, or conversion improvements.

Use the configured price book as the source of truth. If a real price has not been set, display **Price not set** and request a scoped quote. Do not put example prices into customer-facing proposals.

Finance-operations automation remains a later, separately scoped offer. Do not handle client financial data until access controls, retention, auditability, incident handling, and data-processing terms have been reviewed.

## What Reachmark does and does not promise

### We do
- Record public-source provenance and observable facts.
- Distinguish a measured observation from an inference or recommendation.
- Help prioritize opportunities and prepare draft commercial artifacts.
- Preserve human approval for outbound communication.
- Track actual proposals, contracts, invoices, payments, and delivery records separately.
- Use empty values when data is unknown.

### We do not
- Invent contact details, testimonials, performance improvements, prices, or customer outcomes.
- Claim every listed website is broken or that a business is losing a specific amount of revenue without evidence.
- Treat clicks, leads, reports, drafts, or proposals as revenue.
- Automatically send messages or accept contractual commitments without authorization.
- Mark an invoice paid without payment confirmation.
- combine different currencies into one revenue total.

## Minimum client experience

### Before work
- Confirm the client's goal and the decision-maker.
- Send a written scope with deliverables, exclusions, timeline, price/currency, payment terms, and revision limits.
- Record the accepted scope and relevant client approvals.
- Confirm required assets, access, domain ownership, and dependencies.

### During work
- Send a concise progress update on the agreed cadence.
- Flag blockers and changes before they affect the timeline.
- Obtain written approval for scope changes.
- Keep client credentials out of notes, reports, and source control; use least-privilege access.

### At handover
- Confirm deliverables against acceptance criteria.
- Provide final files, links, documentation, and access-transfer instructions.
- Confirm any remaining balance and close out the invoice.
- Ask for feedback and permission before publishing a testimonial or case study.
- Record a real outcome and its evidence source; do not fabricate a metric.

## Solo-operator weekly rhythm

**Monday — choose the work**
- Review unpaid invoices, active projects, replies awaiting action, and qualified opportunities.
- Select one primary niche or segment for the week's prospecting.
- Set realistic targets for verified prospects, reviewed reports, approved outreach, proposals, and paid work.

**Daily — run the loop**
- Check replies and opt-outs first.
- Verify a small batch of real prospects.
- Review evidence before preparing outreach.
- Approve and send only relevant, compliant messages.
- Update next action and due date after each meaningful interaction.

**Friday — inspect outcomes**
- Count actual paid invoices by currency.
- Review proposals, reply rate, win rate, time to payment, and delivery load.
- Separate measured results from missing data.
- Keep, change, or stop the niche/offer based on recorded evidence.

## Scoreboard definitions

- **Qualified prospect:** a real business that matches the intended customer and has a relevant, evidenced opportunity.
- **Verified opportunity:** a stored report with evidence; not necessarily a buyer.
- **Approved send:** a message explicitly reviewed and authorized by a human.
- **Proposal:** a persisted proposal record; not a contract or project.
- **Won / paid:** use the actual agreed business state and invoice payment status, not a positive reply.
- **Delivered:** the project is marked delivered after handover.
- **Revenue:** sum paid invoice amounts separately by currency. Never compare or add NGN, USD, EUR, GBP, or other currencies without an explicit conversion rate and date.
