# Reachmark Client Delivery Playbook

Use this checklist for each paid website, visibility, or digital-growth engagement. Copy it into the project record and mark only work that has actually happened.

## 1. Qualification
- [ ] Business identity and decision-maker confirmed.
- [ ] Customer problem described in the client's words.
- [ ] Relevant website/listing evidence reviewed.
- [ ] Desired outcome and baseline recorded where measurable.
- [ ] Budget and decision timing discussed without pressure.
- [ ] Fit, risks, dependencies, and reasons not to proceed recorded.

## 2. Proposal and agreement
- [ ] Scope and deliverables are explicit.
- [ ] Exclusions and third-party costs are explicit.
- [ ] Price and currency are set from the real quote.
- [ ] Timeline and client dependencies are written down.
- [ ] Revision allowance and change-request process are defined.
- [ ] Payment milestones, due dates, and late-payment handling are stated.
- [ ] Ownership, licensing, access, privacy, and termination terms are covered.
- [ ] Client has accepted the scope in writing.

## 3. Onboarding
- [ ] Welcome message and primary contact confirmed.
- [ ] Kickoff goals and success criteria documented.
- [ ] Required content, brand assets, approvals, and access requested.
- [ ] Access granted through least-privilege accounts; no passwords in chat or notes.
- [ ] Project milestones and update cadence agreed.
- [ ] Invoice issued and payment status confirmed according to the agreement.

## 4. Delivery
- [ ] Work follows the accepted scope.
- [ ] Mobile and desktop layouts reviewed.
- [ ] Forms, links, navigation, and agreed integrations tested.
- [ ] Accessibility and performance checks completed where in scope.
- [ ] Client review and revision rounds recorded.
- [ ] Scope changes approved before additional work begins.
- [ ] Progress update sent on the agreed cadence.

## 5. Handover and closeout
- [ ] Acceptance criteria checked with the client.
- [ ] Final URL/files and handover documentation provided.
- [ ] Ownership and access transferred or confirmed.
- [ ] Backup/export and rollback details supplied where applicable.
- [ ] Final invoice reconciled; only verified payments marked Paid.
- [ ] Project marked Delivered only after handover.
- [ ] Feedback requested.
- [ ] Testimonial or case-study permission obtained before publication.
- [ ] Real outcomes, evidence source, and observation date recorded.
- [ ] Maintenance or next-step offer discussed only if relevant.

## 6. Change request record

For each change, record: requested change, reason, impact on price, impact on timeline, approval date, and updated acceptance criteria. Do not silently expand scope.

## 7. Incident and data handling

If a credential, customer file, or personal data is exposed or sent to the wrong recipient, stop the affected workflow, revoke or rotate access where possible, preserve relevant audit details, notify the responsible owner, and follow the applicable contract and legal requirements. Never put secrets in GitHub issues, commits, screenshots, or project notes.
